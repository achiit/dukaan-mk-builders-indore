Our story

Five years, one street corner, and a lot of finished homes.

We started M.K Builders and Property Associate right here in Nipaniya Gram, in a small office in front of Devine Green, because too many families in this area were getting half-finished promises from contractors who disappeared after the foundation. Our founder grew up watching his own father supervise a family construction business, and that habit of checking every beam and every bill personally never left.

Five years and dozens of plots later, that's still how we work — someone from our team is on site, not just on the phone. Whether it's a two-floor family home or a small commercial block, we treat it the way we'd want our own relative's house built.
